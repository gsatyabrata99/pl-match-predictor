How it works

1

Minutes

How often the player has played, and played 60+ minutes, over his team's last five gameweeks, times FPL's availability flag for injuries and suspensions. Blank gameweeks don't count against him.

2

Output per minute

Season xG, xA, saves, bonus and yellow cards per minute, plus how often he reaches the defensive-contribution threshold (). Small samples are pulled toward the average for his position, so two lucky games don't make a star.

3

Fixture difficulty from the match model

Attacking output scales with his team's expected goals in each fixture compared with its average. Clean-sheet and goals-conceded chances come straight from the match predictor's scoreline grid, so a 2.1 xG team facing a leaky defence is treated very differently from a trip to the league's best.

4

Official scoring

The points per goal, assist, clean sheet and so on are read from the FPL game itself every day, so rule changes are picked up automatically.
